Logs may include the following sensitive information taken at the time of each request to Siri over the past 24 hours:
- The text of the request
- The foreground app
- Which apps are installed on the device
- Your current location
- People, apps, dates, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, and URLs that are on screen
- Output from any AppIntents used to satisfy the request
- The view hierarchy of what is on the screen
- The entities that were detected on screen

You can review the collected content in the attachments before submitting your radar.
